# Moto3 22-0 Draft

**X-0 Sports Draft #23** — Draft 5 Moto3 legends across 6 eras and try to win every race in a 22-round Grand Prix season.

84 legends. 6 eras. 22 rounds. 1 goal: **22-0**.

## About

Moto3 is the entry-level class in the MotoGP World Championship, contested on identical Honda NSF250RW and KTM RC250GP bikes since 2012. The class has launched the careers of champions like Marc Márquez (via Moto2), Maverick Viñales, Jack Miller, Joan Mir, Jorge Martín, and Pedro Acosta.

This draft game celebrates Moto3 from its 250cc predecessors (Rossi, Pedrosa, Lorenzo, Stoner) to the modern Acosta/Alonso era.

## How It Works

1. Draft 5 Moto3 legends from any era (1990s 250cc → 2026)
2. Race 22 Grand Prix rounds on the 2026 calendar
3. Win every race = **22-0 LEGEND** 🏆
